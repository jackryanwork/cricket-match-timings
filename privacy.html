Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Cricnivo provides cricket schedules, match information, Telegram alerts, and reminder features. This policy explains the limited information used to operate those services.

Information we process

  • Telegram user and chat identifiers needed to operate the bot and deliver requested messages.
  • Your Telegram display name when available, for greetings and administrative new-user notifications.
  • Your selected teams and match reminders.
  • Basic technical information required for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

How we use information

We use this information to show relevant matches, save preferences, deliver reminders and service messages, prevent misuse, and maintain Cricnivo. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that operate Cricnivo, including Telegram, Supabase, GitHub Pages, and domain or network providers. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

Your choices

You may cancel individual reminders inside the Mini App and stop or block the Telegram bot at any time. To request access to or deletion of information associated with your Telegram account, contact us below.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to @MembersHelpDesk on Telegram.
