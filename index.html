FAQs

Good to know
What can I find on CricNivo?

CricNivo shows today's matches, tomorrow's fixtures, upcoming schedules, and recent results from one simple homepage. You can also read the today's cricket match schedule guide.

Are match times shown in my local time?

Yes. Match timings automatically use your browser's local time zone, so you can check cricket fixtures without converting from another time zone.

How does the Favourite button work?

Tap ♡ Favourite on any match to save it on this device and browser. Your saved matches appear under My Favourite Matches in the menu. Tap ♥ Favourite again to remove a match. Favourites are not connected to an account, so clearing browser data or changing devices will remove them.

How do I set a reminder for a match?

Choose Set reminder on a match card, select when you want to be reminded, and save your preference. You can manage saved reminders from the reminder menu.

Does CricNivo show a countdown to each match?

Yes. Upcoming match cards show a live countdown until the scheduled start time. Live matches no longer show a pre-match countdown.

Can I see where a match is being played?

When venue information is available, CricNivo displays the match location on the match card.

Can I use CricNivo from Telegram?

Yes. Open the CricNivo Telegram bot and use its Open App button to access the Mini App.
